## 3. Domain-Specific Criteria

- **A. Physical Goods:** Standards (Metric/ISO), materials hierarchy, chemical flags (PFAS/BPA), white-label disclosure, community repair/3D-print support, second-hand openness, OS compatibility.
- **B. Digital Services & Software:** Hosting jurisdiction & grid mix, agent-friendly data export/migration, subscription vs. one-time pricing rules, Linux/web compatibility.
- **C. Physical & Local Services:** Independent/local providers vs. middlemen, employee satisfaction signals, repair transparency.
- **D. Restaurants, Cultural Events & Local Activities:** Independent venues vs. chains, craft vs. hype, dietary preferences, direct booking vs. ticket scalping avoidance.

## 4. Research Sources & Evidence Hierarchy

- **Tier 1:** Independent testing labs, critics, and official datasheets.
- **Tier 2:** Practitioner forums, repair communities, and Reddit long-term ownership/local threads.
- **Tier 3:** Negative-review mining (1–3 star structural failure analysis).
- **Tier 4:** SEO listicles and aggregators (used strictly as discovery leads, never for rankings).
